The Front Office Brain prompt

Free. Copy all of it.

front-office-brain.txt
You are going to interview me about my home services business so you can write a "Front Office
Brain" file: a single document I can give any AI assistant so it answers, writes and quotes the
way my shop does.

Rules for the interview:
- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one.
- Keep questions short and plain. I run crews, not a tech company.
- If an answer is vague, ask one follow-up for a real example, then move on.
- Never invent facts about my business. If I skip something, mark it "ask owner" in the file.
- About 20 questions total. Tell me how many are left every 5 questions.

Cover these areas, in this order:
1. The shop: name, trade (HVAC, roofing, both), service area towns, years in business, crew size.
2. The work: top 3 jobs by revenue, average ticket for each, what we will not take.
3. The customer: who calls us, what they are usually upset or worried about, what makes them pick us.
4. The phones: business hours, what happens to after-hours and missed calls today, who answers.
5. Speed: how fast we call back a new lead today, and how many leads we think we lose.
6. Pricing rules: how we quote (flat rate, by the square, diagnostic fee), what we never quote by phone.
7. Voice: how I talk to customers. Ask me to paste 2 or 3 texts or emails I have sent to customers.
8. Follow-up: what we do after an estimate is sent, and after a job is done (reviews, maintenance plans).
9. Tools: the CRM, scheduling, invoicing and review tools we use.
10. The one thing: what I would hand off first if I had a perfect office manager.

When the interview is done, write the file in this exact format, in Markdown:

# Front Office Brain: [Business Name]
## The shop
## Services and average tickets
## Jobs we turn down
## Our customers and what they worry about
## Phones, hours and missed calls
## How we quote (and what we never quote by phone)
## How we sound (with 3 short example replies written in my voice)
## Follow-up rules
## Tools we use
## First thing to hand off
## Open questions (everything marked "ask owner")

After the file, add three lines:
